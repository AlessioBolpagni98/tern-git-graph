# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-20

First release.

- Commit graph of every local branch, tag and (toggleable) remote branch, drawn as colored lanes beside a commit table.
- Quick look floating over the focused pane (<kbd>⌃⌥G</kbd>) and a left sidebar that remembers its width.
- Follows the terminal it was opened from and refreshes after commands finish inside the repository.
- Selected commit's details beside the graph: author, message, refs, parents, per-file line counts and diffs; expand to
  the whole pane.
- Branch, ahead/behind and commit count in the head row.
